# M1 reference-period amendment — 27 September 2026

This is a planning amendment, not a new user approval. The original request asked us to choose a consistent reference date or period. The user did not specify September 2026. The plan selected that month and then made current condition an M1 gate. The separate pending forest question is withdrawn because that gate was introduced by our plan. It is not recorded as an accepted answer.

M1 now uses the measured **2022 environmental baseline**: City LiDAR from 7–9 September 2022 and reference imagery from 6 June–1 July 2022. Canopy remains the measured 2022 blockout. Current source-backed cycling routes, access modes, directions and restrictions form a separately dated overlay. Later landmark references retain their individual dates and uncertainty. This is an explicit data contract, not a claim that every feature matches one historic instant or that 2022 access rules were the same.

The 10 forest-treatment review areas, source links and unresolved opening geometry remain in `manifests/forest-condition-overrides.json`. No crown was deleted and no whole treatment polygon was cleared. No September 2026 or current-forest completeness claim is made. Treatment counts still cannot be converted into removed crowns. September 2026 vegetation reconstruction is later work and requires usable dated, locatable evidence before it can support a current-condition claim.

This change removes the plan-created current-forest source gate from M1. It does not waive an existing visible route defect, missing route surface, physical obstruction, collision failure, scale/axis check, fixed source review, visual inspection, full ride or performance trace. Numerical tolerances are unchanged. The separate user decision to keep absolute accuracy unverified remains exactly as recorded in `M1-MEASUREMENT-EXCEPTIONS.md`.

Plan §13.2 and the active source/status records now point to `manifests/m1-reference-period.json`. The old question and old September 2026 proposal remain in the history; they are superseded by this explicit planning decision. M1 remains open until the other acceptance gates pass.
