# M1 restroom and entrance crosswalk

Status: source and geometry package, 27 September 2026. This work did not operate Blender, Unreal or the standalone application. M1 remains open.

`manifests/m1-restroom-entrance-crosswalk.json` retains all 20 OSM toilet tags within the City park boundary. These are not 20 unique buildings. Seventeen tags lie within or within 1 m of existing survey roof forms. Their coarse form coverage is recorded separately from room identity. The two Lumberman tags identify two rooms in one below-road complex. The Nature House building is assigned to the named-building package and is not duplicated here.

Railway toilet way88279372 lies in survey cover `lidar2022_490000_5461000_3`. That existing mass is the M1 restroom form. The Prospect cafe compound includes a southern washroom wing, way88295811, within cover `lidar2022_489000_5462000_1`; the separate Prospect picnic/parking toilet is cover `lidar2022_489000_5461000_2`. A nearest named candidate is a cross-reference, not independent room identification.

The three functional entrance connections are City junctions 007, 035 and 045. The crosswalk includes their marker data and 41 barrier records within 15 m of the main circuit. Those records include walls, curbs, gates and duplicate/adjacent tags. They are not 41 missing gate models. Preserve the route graph's legal mode and grade separation. A physical entrance arch is not inferred from a marker.

## Lumberman facility

The City 2018 site plan A0.03 places the male and female facilities under Stanley Park Drive, on either side of a pedestrian underpass. The City2025 report's existing-condition photo shows the stone facades, green upper panels, open central passage and road parapet. The 2022 ortho and LiDAR distinguish the raised road from both lower forecourts. Separate above-ground roof boxes at the OSM amenity points would be incorrect.

The new package has 62 meshes and 744 triangles. It contains a separate lower floor, road deck, two facade wings, side returns, open passage walls, closed facility doors and coarse parapet rails. There are no room interiors. The source plane fits use 3,579 road returns and 1,572 ground inliers. RMS is 0.0241 m for the road and 0.0239 m for the floor. At the local frame origin, floor height is 2.74079 m and road height is 6.29072 m CGVD2013. These are source-fit statistics, not independent absolute accuracy.

The 4.0 m passage width, 0.65 m slab depth, facade span, concealed walls, panel arrangement and rail sizes are explicit M1 estimates. The temporary 4.0 m access label in the site plan is **not** used as an as-built tunnel measurement. The model's estimated minimum vertical clearance is 2.8703 m.

The package is generated by `pipeline/gis/build_lumberman_facilities.py`; its editable parameters are `manifests/lumberman-facility-settings.json`. `manifests/lumberman-facility-blockouts.json` retains every input hash, both fitted planes, floor/roof corner controls and required integration actions. `pipeline/blender/build_lumberman_facilities.py` stages the package without editing terrain.

Root must cut and replace the three explicit regions in `data/derived/lumberman-facilities/replacement-footprints-local.geojson`. The lower floor fills the full 28 m by 19 m skewed footprint. The road deck lies wholly within it. The central passage is 4 m wide. Preserve the seawall cycling pavement, which is a different path. The boundary-closure stage must join the lower floor and raised road to their respective neighbours. The terrain must not remain across the passage.

Required live evidence: a front view from the waterpark, both pedestrian portals, a walk through the passage, a traversal above it, floor-edge/road-edge views and a retained contact trace. Numerical geometry and offline source panels do not close those checks.
