The OSM-derived source and adapted databases in SourceData/Pedestrian,
SourceData/PublicSpaces, SourceData/Navigation, SourceData/Skyline,
SourceData/NorthShore, SourceData/Facilities and SourceData/PhysicalGates are
provided under ODbL 1.0. Keep the contributor notice, licence URL and source
records with copies of these databases. These notices do not change the
licence of the simulation code or independently authored art.

Each group preserves project-relative paths. JSON and GeoJSON records contain
feature IDs, positions, source associations, estimates and transformations.
OSM XML files retain their original tags and edit timestamps. NPZ files are
ZIP containers of NumPy arrays: editable vertices, faces, anchors and, where
used, colours. Only mesh files named in the current group manifest are copied.
source-bundle-index.json records the copied project paths and SHA-256 hashes.
The public bundle uses these grouped project-relative paths.

Pedestrian: walking graph, estimated widths, source XML, current derived
surface database, geometry and generation scripts.

PublicSpaces: unedited source polygons, local authoring polygons, patch and
exclusion records, current terrain-conforming geometry and scripts. Hidden
mixed-site boundaries are references, not completed surfaces.

Navigation: original park XML, inland restriction graph, OSM-assisted branch
direction review, functional marker records and geometry. Independent City
route data remains identified separately. These are estimated blockout signs,
not surveyed physical signs or a statement of current legal access.

Skyline: four bounded OSM XML extracts dated 27 September 2026, the current
building-part database, tagged and estimated dimensions, generated geometry,
source ledgers and transformation scripts. The City 2009 building extract
and its height records retain their separate Open Government Licence -
Vancouver notice. Historical data does not establish present-day dimensions.

NorthShore: four bounded OSM XML extracts dated 27 September 2026, seven
industrial site envelopes, source associations, tagged and estimated forms,
current editable geometry and scripts. The operator and contractor pages
and PDFs remain research references. No PDF or photograph is included.

Facilities: original park XML, the toilet/entrance crosswalk, Lumberman
below-road facility pose and estimates, editable geometry, source hashes,
replacement footprints and generation scripts. City LiDAR-derived height
planes keep their Vancouver Open Government Licence provenance. The City
tender drawing and gate-report photographs are reference only. Source
room tags do not establish opening hours or independently surveyed dimensions.

PhysicalGates: original park XML, dated existing-condition gate placement,
estimated dimensions, physical mesh frames and separate walking path records.
Current field condition is not certified. The City report photographs are not
included. The source geometry and the derived source database remain editable.

LagoonStructures, RouteSculptures and AirIndia preserve the associated City
survey derivatives, OSM-assisted placement, source records and scripts. The
City terrain/survey geometry uses OGL-Vancouver; OSM-derived records use ODbL.
Artwork registry metadata does not grant artwork reproduction rights. These
are limited development envelopes, not licensed finished artwork replicas.
All source photos, inscriptions, reference PDF pages and logos are excluded.

Other contents: City terrain/height content contains information licensed
under the Open Government Licence - Vancouver. Regional terrain and geoid
references retain the separate BC and Canada notices in DATA-ATTRIBUTION.txt.
The arrays contain the resulting geometry; the entire upstream LiDAR/raster
archive is not bundled. Referenced upstream source URLs and hashes remain in
the manifests. The source bundle is not a complete engine development kit.
