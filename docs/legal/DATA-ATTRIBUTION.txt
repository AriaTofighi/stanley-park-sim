Stanley Park simulation — Seawall preview release

This build contains modified public geospatial data. It is not a City of
Vancouver, Province of British Columbia, or Government of Canada product.
These agencies do not endorse it. The scene is not a navigation aid.

The Blender source remains editable. No third-party photo texture library,
scanned landmark pack, or wildlife animation pack is included.
The Seawall tree/bark/leaf textures, gulls, explorer model and animation,
procedural shore objects, and bicycle rider animation are original project
work. The public repository and asset archive include their editable source and authoring scripts.

Lagoon structures, coarse route sculpture envelopes and the Air India wall
use City2022 survey geometry under OGL-Vancouver plus separately identified
OSM placement records under ODbL1.0. See SourceData/LagoonStructures,
SourceData/RouteSculptures and SourceData/AirIndia. City art-registry metadata
does not grant artwork rights. No carving textures, source photographs,
inscriptions, logos or reference PDF pages are incorporated or distributed.
The limited development geometry is not an assertion of permission for a
finished artwork replica or public release.

Named ground-space additions: original OSM golf, garden and railway geometry
is offered under ODbL 1.0 in SourceData/GroundSpaces. Image-picked ground
boundaries contain City2022 orthophoto information under OGL-Vancouver.
Source images are not distributed as textures. Approximate project envelopes,
rail widths and the biofilter local water-level proxy are identified in the
source records. The separate source package does not certify current conditions.

Railway station/Cob House and Nature House threshold geometry use City survey
information under OGL-Vancouver. See SourceData/RailwayStructures and
SourceData/NatureHouseThreshold. The City2015 railway tender (AMD No.1,
19 June 2015) and operator photographs were research references only. The
architect's PDF sheets and photographs are excluded from distribution.

Lumberman terrain transitions and closure geometry use City2022 survey data
and the separately identified OSM path alignment. See SourceData/LumbermanClosures.
The source package retains distinct upper-road and lower-walkway controls.

Finite site-completion grounds and original OSM feature links are offered in
SourceData/SiteCompletions under ODbL1.0, with City2022 survey/image derivatives
under OGL-Vancouver. Existing terrain and canopy are linked without duplicate
monuments. Local pond water levels are explicit unmeasured proxies. The two
coarse low play envelopes are in SourceData/WaterparkLowForms under OGL-Vancouver.

SourceData/TotemPrecinct contains limited pole/portal envelopes, source metadata
and City survey derivatives. Some concealed envelopes are bounded estimates.
Artwork, photographs, carving, paint patterns and inscriptions are not copied.
The original artwork rights remain separate from City open-data permissions.

The finite branch-join fixtures in SourceData/Navigation retain City route
geometry and the separately recorded OSM direction review. They add no new
external source or licence and do not establish runtime behavior.

The closing-seam support delta is in SourceData/Navigation. It contains the
original project helper and caller, exact live-authored vertices/faces,
unchanged source pavement/support NPZ files, controls and the retained failure.
The derivative adds five vertices and four faces only to Blender
SM_PavementSupport_049; its base NPZ is unchanged. City survey derivatives
retain OGL-Vancouver terms. No research image or document is included.
