# Data and credits

The scene combines measured historical terrain with authored scenery. The main near-terrain and canopy reference is City of Vancouver data from 2022. Other terrain and map sources have their own dates. This is not a current survey or a navigation product.

Original models, textures, animation, and procedural scene work were created for this project. Reference photographs, artwork images, engineering PDFs, and restricted survey reports are excluded from the public downloads. No engine source or editor installation is distributed.

The v0.5 forest loop is **Forest Ambience** by **TinyWorlds**, published under [CC0](https://opengameart.org/content/forest-ambience). Its source, conversion, and hashes are in [nature-audio.json](../manifests/nature-audio.json). It is general artistic ambience, not a Stanley Park field recording.

The new ferns and salal use the [Stanley Park Ecology Society State of the Park report](https://stanleyparkecology.ca/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/SOPEI-Full-2010.pdf) as a plant reference. Their meshes are original, simplified artwork. Random ground cover, flower patches, visitor activity, and picnic locations are artistic additions rather than surveyed placements.

## Source databases

The Windows ZIP includes `SourceData`: the registered OpenStreetMap extracts, adapted databases, editable geometry arrays, source notices, and file index. These machine-readable data sources are also available in the separate `StanleyPark-SourceData-v0.5.1.zip` on the [release page](https://github.com/AriaTofighi/stanley-park-sim/releases/tag/v0.5.1).

The OSM-derived databases are offered under ODbL 1.0. Other inputs keep their separately stated licences. `manifests/source-distribution.json` lists the included data groups. Source dates, transformations, and estimate limits remain in their individual manifests.

Open data permissions do not grant separate rights in depicted artwork. Carvings, paint patterns, inscriptions, source photographs, and logos are not copied into the distributed assets.
